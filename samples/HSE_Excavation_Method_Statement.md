# Deep Excavation and Shoring — Method Statement

## Scope

This method statement covers excavation deeper than 1.5 m for foundations,
basements and utility trenches on all company projects in Abu Dhabi.

## Permits and Approvals

No excavation shall start before the following are obtained:

* Permit to Excavate issued by the HSE Department, valid for a maximum of 7 days
* NOC from all utility authorities (ADDC, ADNOC, Etisalat, Du, ADSSC)
* Approved shoring design stamped by a registered structural engineer
* Confirmation of utility scanning (GPR) performed within the last 14 days

## Excavation Requirements

### Side Slopes and Shoring

| Soil type | Max unsupported depth | Required slope | Shoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solid rock | 5.0 m | Vertical | Not required |
| Stiff clay | 1.5 m | 1:0.75 | Above 1.5 m |
| Loose sand | 1.2 m | 1:1.5 | Above 1.2 m |
| Made ground / fill | 1.0 m | 1:2 | Always |

### Edge Protection

Guardrails shall be installed around every excavation deeper than **2.0 m**,
set back a minimum of **1.0 m** from the edge. Excavated material shall be
stockpiled no closer than **1.5 m** from the excavation edge.

### Access

A ladder, ramp or stairway shall be provided within **7.5 m** of any worker
inside the excavation. Ladders shall extend **1.0 m** above the landing level.

## Inspection Regime

The excavation shall be inspected by a competent person:

1. Before each shift starts
2. After any rainfall, vibration or loading event
3. After any damage to the shoring system
4. Following any collapse or movement of the sides

Inspection findings are recorded on form **HSE-F-022** and retained for the
duration of the project plus 3 years.

## Emergency Response

In the event of a collapse:

```text
1. Stop all work and evacuate the excavation.
2. Do NOT enter the excavation to attempt rescue.
3. Call the Project Emergency Number and Civil Defence (997).
4. Notify the HSE Manager and Project Manager immediately.
5. Preserve the scene until the investigation team arrives.
```

## Training

All operatives working in or around excavations must hold a valid
**Confined Space and Excavation Safety** certificate renewed every 2 years.
